Binge TV Player: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Binge TV Player ("the app") is a streaming client for Samsung TVs. It contains no content of its own. You connect your own sources, and the app plays from them. This policy explains what the app stores and what it sends to others.

The developer, thedarapu, does not run any server that receives your data and has no way to see it. There are no developer analytics, advertising or tracking in the app.

What the app stores on your TV

These stay in the app's local storage on your TV. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

  • The addresses and credentials of the sources you add: a Jellyfin server and/or a portal address and its MAC address.
  • API keys and sign-in tokens you provide for TMDB, Trakt and TheIntroDB.
  • Your watch progress, watchlist and viewing history, and cached titles and artwork.
  • Your app preferences.

What the app sends, and to whom

The app connects only to the services you set up. It sends data to these parties:

  • Your own sources (Jellyfin server or portal). The app sends your credentials, your device's portal MAC address and requests for the titles you browse and play. These servers are run by you or by whoever you chose. Their handling of your data is governed by their own policies, not this one.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org). The app asks for artwork, cast and descriptions for titles you view. TMDB receives the title identifiers, your API key and your IP address. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Trakt (trakt.tv). Only if you sign in. The app sends what you watched, your playback progress and your watchlist changes to your own Trakt account, and reads them back. Trakt's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • TheIntroDB (theintrodb.org). The app looks up intro, recap and credits times using a movie's or show's public identifier (and the season and episode for a show). Your IP address is visible to the service. It is not linked to your account.
  • The setup relay (optional). If you use the "Set up from your phone" QR code, the details you type on your phone pass through a relay server to the TV. They are encrypted so that only your TV can read them. Setup sessions are held in memory and expire after 10 minutes. The relay is run by you or by whoever you point the app to.

Like any internet service, each of these can see your IP address when you connect.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and we do not have a server that receives it.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not use the TV's camera or microphone, or read your contacts, location or other apps.
  • The app only streams. It does not download or store media.

Your choices

  • You can remove any source, key or Trakt connection in the app's Settings, which deletes it from the TV.
  • You can revoke the app's access to Trakt from your Trakt account settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your TV.
  • To delete data held by TMDB, Trakt, TheIntroDB or your own sources, contact those services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. What you can watch depends on the sources you add.
